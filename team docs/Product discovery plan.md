Product Discovery & User Insight Plan
Phase 1: Team Preparation
	•	Train two team members as facilitators via shadowing
	•	Lead facilitator (you) runs the first user session while the two others observe
	•	Trainees then lead subsequent sessions with you observing
	•	Goal: establish three capable facilitators ready for ongoing cadence
Phase 2: Kickoff Workshop
	•	Bring the full team together for a structured session
	•	Synthesize existing knowledge about your target users (mainstream Americans)
	•	Map out core user goals and the “happy path” interactions with the product
	•	Identify your biggest assumptions and unknowns
	•	Facilitated by your internal team — keeps ownership and builds capability
Phase 3: Continuous Discovery
	•	Each week, some facilitators conducts a structured conversation with a real user
	•	Rotate facilitators to distribute the workload and build skills across the team
	•	Focus each session on understanding the user’s perspective, workflow, and pain points
	•	Capture insights consistently so patterns emerge over time
Phase 4: User Journey Mapping
	•	After early discovery sessions, synthesize insights into user journey maps
	•	Document what users are trying to accomplish at each step
	•	Identify friction points, emotional highs and lows, and unmet needs
	•	Involve your UX team members in translating insights into journey diagrams
Phase 5: Roadmap Development
	•	Use journey maps and discovery insights to prioritize features and interactions
	•	Focus on what delivers the most value to the user first
	•	Review and update the roadmap regularly as new discovery insights come in
	•	Treat the roadmap as a living document, not a fixed plan
Guiding Principles
	•	Research first, build second — never assume what users want
	•	Continuous discovery keeps the team honest and responsive as the product evolves
	•	Internal facilitators build long-term team capability and ownership​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
